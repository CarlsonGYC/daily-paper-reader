## 摘要
本文在熵正则化松弛控制框架下，研究了具有输入延迟的无限时域经典随机最优控制问题。特别地，通过构造一个松弛系统并引入熵正则化项，我们将经典最优控制问题重新表述为熵正则化形式，并推导出遵循高斯分布的最优控制器。此外，我们证明了当探索权重趋于零时，最优高斯控制分布收敛于最优狄拉克测度。最后，通过数值仿真验证了所提出方法的有效性。

## Abstract
This paper investigates the infinite-horizon classical stochastic optimal control problem with input delay under an entropy-regularized relaxed control framework. In particular, by constructing a relaxed system and introducing an entropy regularization term, we reformulate the classical optimal control problem into an entropy regularized formulation, and derive the optimal controller that follows a Gaussian distribution. Furthermore, we show that the optimal Gaussian control distribution converges to the optimal Dirac measure as the exploration weight tends to zero. Numerical simulation is provided to validate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
